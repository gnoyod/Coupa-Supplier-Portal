First time logging in

Registering for the Coupa Supplier Network (CSN)

Do this once, before anything else.

  1. Check your inbox for the Coupa Supplier Portal invitation email
  2. Click Join Coupa Supplier Portal
  3. Enter your password and name
  4. Click Create an account
  5. Go back to your inbox and find the email verification code
  6. Copy the code, paste it in, then click Next

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Enablement

Enabling multi-factor authentication

Set this up before managing remit-to addresses — you'll need a verification code from here.

  1. Go to Legal Entities under Business Profile
  2. Click Enable
  3. Choose your authentication method — authenticator app, text, or passkey
  4. Enter the 6-digit code
  5. Click Enable
Note — email isn't available as an authentication method for Qualtrics accounts.

Profile and admin items

Adding additional users

Use this to give a colleague their own login.

  1. Click Setup in the main menu
  2. Click Invite User
  3. Enter their name and email
  4. Choose their permissions
  5. Click Send Invitation

Managing remit-to addresses

  1. Go to Business Profile, then Legal Entities
  2. Enter the 6-digit verification code from your MFA method
  3. Click Create
  4. Fill in the information
  5. Click Save
  6. Click Continue to add new payment methods
  7. Click Add Payment Method
  8. Select the legal entity
  9. Fill in the form
  10. Click Save
  11. Add the payment method to your Qualtrics profile

Submitting an invoice

Submitting an invoice

  1. Log in to the portal
  2. Click Orders in the main menu
  3. Find the PO you want to invoice
  4. Click the gold coin icon, or open the PO and click Create Invoice
  5. Enter your invoice number
  6. Check that quantities, prices, and taxes are correct
  7. Attach a PDF copy of your invoice, if required
  8. Click Submit
Getting an error? If the PO is out of funds, email your Qualtrics contact for that PO and cc Procurement.

Resolving a disputed invoice

  1. Click Invoices in the main menu
  2. Find the invoice marked Disputed in the Status column
  3. Click the invoice number, or the Resolve icon under Actions
  4. Read the dispute reason at the top of the invoice
  5. Choose Correct Invoice to fix a detail and resubmit, or Void Invoice if it's a duplicate
  6. Make your correction
  7. Click Submit
Before you correct — check our AP team's note for the dispute reason. If it's a PO balance issue, email your Qualtrics contact for that PO and cc Procurement.
